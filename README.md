# ✦ Mimo Arcade

**A tiny world of games, pixels, and little adventures.**

Welcome to **Mimo Arcade**, a collection of playful mini-games starring Mimo — a squishy, expressive little pixel mascot who's always ready for another adventure.

Explore 10 mini-games, discover hidden story quests, beat your high scores, and see what Mimo gets up to along the way.
**🎮 Play Mimo Arcade:**  
https://crystal-shine.github.io/Mimo-Arcade/
---
## 🌸 What's Inside?

Mimo Arcade brings together quick challenges, puzzles, memory games, and interactive adventures in one browser-based experience.
### 🎮 Mini-Games
| Game | Description |
|---|---|
| 💗 Heart Catcher | Catch hearts and stars while avoiding rainy clouds. |
| ☁️ Fluffy Flight | Float through the clouds and navigate tricky gaps. |
| 🧠 Memory Match | Flip cards and find all matching pairs. |
| 🔢 Merge Tiles | Slide and combine matching numbers. |
| 🧩 Slide Puzzle | Rearrange the tiles to restore the original picture. |
| 🍓 Berry Caterpillar | Guide a hungry caterpillar on its berry hunt. |
| 🐾 Peek-a-Mimo | Catch Mimo popping out for a surprise visit. |
| ✨ Sparkle Echo | Remember colour sequences and repeat them correctly. |
| 🌙 Night Lights | Switch off every light to complete the puzzle. |
| 🌅 Sunrise Jigsaw | Piece together a beautiful sunrise scene. |

### 🔐 Secret Story Quests
There's more to Mimo Arcade than mini-games.
Two hidden story adventures unlock when your combined best scores reach 100 points. Each game contributes up to 40 points.
**🌙 Moonberry Mission**  
A glowing berry needs to reach Grandma's lantern before dawn. Can you make it in time?
**🚂 Starlight Express**  
A race across town begins as Mimo tries to catch the last train.
Your decisions influence Mimo's energy and hope. Some choices are timed, making every decision count.

## ✨ Features
- **Pixel-art aesthetic:** Chunky pixel borders, pixel-style typography, and a playful visual identity.
- **Meet Mimo:** An interactive mascot with reactions, speech bubbles, and expressive animations.
- **Sound & Music:** Browser-generated sound effects and background music using the Web Audio API.
- **Custom Settings:** Switch sound and music on or off.
- **Multiple Controls:** Play using touch, mouse, or keyboard.
- **Responsive Design:** Adapted for phones, tablets, and desktop screens.
- **Progress Tracking:** Best scores and progress are saved locally in your browser.
- **Light & Dark Modes:** Automatically follows your device's appearance preference.

## 🚀 Run Locally
Want to explore the project on your own computer?
1. Download `index.html` from this repository.
2. Open the file in any modern web browser.
3. Start playing!
No installation, dependencies, or build tools required.
---
## 🔒 Privacy
Mimo Arcade is designed to work without user accounts.
- No account registration.
- No analytics.
- No cookies.
- Game scores and settings are stored in your browser's local storage.
- Progress can be cleared through **Settings → Reset Progress**.
The game also includes Terms and Privacy pages accessible through its top menu.
